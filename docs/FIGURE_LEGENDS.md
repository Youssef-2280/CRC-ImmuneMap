# Figure legends

File locations: `figures/main/` (PNG only). See `docs/FIGURE_INDEX.md` for all filenames and supplementary plots.

**Figure 1 — Discovery cross-program associations.** Three prespecified patient-level Spearman correlations from GSE132465 among 11 complete-case tumor patients. Labels display correlation coefficient and Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted permutation p-value. No association met conventional statistical significance.

**Figure 2 — Matched validation scores.** Each line represents one GSE144735 patient with >=20 eligible cells in both Normal and pooled Tumor Core+Border for that particular program. Points show patient mean UCell scores, paired by patient, not pseudoreplicated cells. Patient eligibility differs by program; y-axis scales may differ across subplots.

**Figure 3 — Validation paired differences across anatomical regions.** Dots are individual eligible patient tumor-minus-matched-normal differences, with short horizontal lines depicting program/region medians. Deltas for Tumor Core, Tumor Border, and combined Core+Border are alternative within-patient contrasts; the regions are not independent cohorts. All panels descriptive.

**Figure 4 — Validation sensitivity to inclusion rules.** Heatmap cells show median paired tumor-minus-normal UCell delta (top) and eligible matched patient count (bottom) for combined Core+Border, with minimum 20 eligible cells in tumor and Normal. Reducing high-risk clusters reduces patient coverage; heatmap hues should not be interpreted as standardized cross-program effect sizes.

**Figure 5 — Three-program patient coverage.** Number of eligible primary_07e cell compartments (SPP1_TAM macrophage, FAP_CAF fibroblast, CD8_Dysfunction CD8) with >=20 cells, per patient and tumor region. All three covered for two patients in Tumor Core and three in combined Core+Border. Core and Border are paired regions of the same patients.

**All figures**: derived from original project CSV results and the user-approved `primary_07e` analytical selection. Figure graphics are final-style visual summaries, but biological inference and clinical identity validation remain explicitly limited as documented.
