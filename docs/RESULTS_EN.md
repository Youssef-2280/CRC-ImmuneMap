# CRC-ImmuneMap — Results (scientifically conservative draft)

## Discovery: patient-level cross-program associations

Among 11 complete-case tumor patients from GSE132465, SPP1_TAM and FAP_CAF scores showed a positive but non-significant patient-level trend (Spearman ρ = 0.400; permutation p = 0.223; BH-adjusted p = 0.669). SPP1_TAM and CD8_Dysfunction exhibited little association (ρ = 0.082; permutation p = 0.81685; adjusted p = 0.81685). FAP_CAF and CD8_Dysfunction exhibited a weak inverse trend (ρ = −0.182; permutation p = 0.596; adjusted p = 0.81685). Thus **none of the three prespecified cross-program associations were statistically supported** in the analyzed Discovery group (Figure 1).

## Validation: matched tumor–normal program differences

The second patient cohort, GSE144735 (six patients from the same published study), was analyzed using the prespecified UCell signatures and the approved `primary_07e` analysis-selection policy. With a minimum of 20 eligible cells on both compared sides, mean SPP1_TAM scores were higher in combined Tumor Core+Border than matched Normal tissue in **4/4** evaluable patients (median paired delta **+0.344**). FAP_CAF scores were higher in **6/6** (median **+0.228**). CD8_Dysfunction scores were higher in **3/3** (median **+0.027**), whereas CD8_Cytotoxicity scores were higher in **2/3** (median **+0.024**) (Figures 2–3).

These comparisons are **descriptive**, not confirmatory inferential tests, and denominators differ between programs. In the validation cohort, complete three-compartment tumor coverage was available for only **2/6** patients in Core and **3/6** in combined Core+Border at the 20-cell cutoff (Figure 5), preventing a meaningful independent test of the proposed SPP1–FAP–CD8 triad.

## Sensitivity and interpretation

Positive paired SPP1_TAM and FAP_CAF directions were retained among evaluable patients after cross-lineage exclusion. Omission of predefined high-risk clusters reduced SPP1_TAM eligible matched patients from **four to two** (Core+Border) and reduced the FAP_CAF median paired difference from approximately **+0.228 to +0.095**; these changes highlight the impact of selection and donor effects (Figure 4). CD8_Dysfunction was more sensitive to inclusion assumptions. Taken together, the data suggest **tumor-associated elevation of macrophage SPP1 and fibroblast FAP expression programs in covered patient comparisons**. They **do not demonstrate** cross-program covariance, cellular interactions, a causal axis, or independent-study replication.

> **Recommended one-sentence conclusion:** *Tumor-associated SPP1 macrophage and FAP fibroblast expression programs showed directionally consistent increases in matched tumor tissue, whereas patient-level SPP1–FAP–CD8 dysfunction coupling was not established.*
